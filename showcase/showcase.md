# Deepcore

**Depth is cheap. Height is expensive.** Stranded at a derelict mining outpost, you have a drill, a jetpack, and one way off a dead planet: build your own escape rocket. Everything it needs is somewhere beneath your boots.

Cut a shaft through the earth, follow a glimmer of mineral through the rock, and pack the cargo bay. Then make the decision that matters: one more vein, or the climb home? Every kilogram makes your jetpack work harder. Every second underground spends the fuel you need to see the sky again.

The outpost is your lifeline. Sell your haul, repair the pressure suit, strengthen the drill, and buy the lift to bring deeper riches home. Save your progress at the pad. Seven upgrade tracks and a kit of explosives, teleporters, and emergency supplies let you prepare for the next descent your own way.

Below the gray rock lie pale gas seeps, unbreakable boulders, molten pools, and crystals your scanner can barely hear. Find Resonite. Find Cryenite. Reach the planet’s Core—and bring its unstable Sample back before ninety seconds run out. Five components. One launch. A very long way home.
